Voice of customer NLP Sentiment
Sample

Thousands of free-text complaints, ranked by what to fix first

Open-text feedback is the most honest data a company has and the least used, because reading it does not scale. This clusters it, scores sentiment, and ranks issues by frequency against how badly they land. Volume alone picks the wrong target and anger alone picks a rare one.
